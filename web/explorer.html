Sources, definitions & coverage

Dollar values: Census ACS 1-year B25077 is the median value of owner-occupied housing units, based on reported home values rather than transaction prices. B19013 is median household income. Dollar amounts use each survey year’s dollars. Cards retain Census margins of error. A ratio compares the two medians; it is not a mortgage-payment affordability measure.

Coverage: National and state/D.C. dollar comparisons cover 2005–2019 and 2021–2024. ACS 1-year values are unavailable here before 2005, and no standard 2020 release was published. Current metro/division code matches vary by year; all 100 match in 2023–2024. Earlier metro boundaries are not harmonized to today’s boundaries. No missing year is interpolated.

Earlier metro income: The Household income view adds 129 verified observations across 27 areas for 1999–2004. Matching requires the same county set in the 1999 and 2023 metro definitions. The 2000–2001 observations are in 2002 or 2003 dollars and appear as separate series. Income for 1991–1998 remains missing. This does not extend the paired ACS home-value ratios.

Growth: FHFA traditional purchase-only, seasonally adjusted HPI is averaged across all four quarters and rebased to the 1991 annual average. Income growth uses the separate historical Census household-income series. It is not spliced with ACS income. Metro income growth is unavailable without a comparable 1991 baseline. The gap is housing growth minus income growth, in percentage points.
